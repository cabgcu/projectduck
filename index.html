    How to Play

    1. 1Find rubber ducks hidden around campus. Rebel Coordinates on the home screen shows how many are still out at each location.
    2. 2Scan the duck's QR code with the in-app scanner, or just with your phone's camera.
    3. 3Earn points for every duck you rescue and climb the leaderboard.

    Code of Conduct

    Rules

    • Each duck can be rescued once. The first rescuer to scan it gets the points.
    • Different ducks are worth different points. Rare ducks are worth more.
    • If a duck "isn't in play yet," it hasn't been activated. Check back later.
    • You can scan up to 10 hologram codes a day. The limit resets at midnight. Rebel ducks have no limit.
    • The leaderboard ranks rescuers by points. Rescuers with equal points share a place.
    • Broken code? Report it from the Info page. Up to 5 Rebel duck and 10 hologram code reports are honored per rescuer.
    • Play fair: one account per student, using your own Student ID.

    Rewards

    Prizes

    The top rescuers on the leaderboard win prizes. Prize details will come through as a transmission, so keep an eye out for the hologram.

    Damaged Intel

    Broken Code?

    If a duck's QR code won't scan or got damaged, report it and you'll get the duck's points automatically. You can have up to 5 Rebel duck and 10 hologram code reports honored.
